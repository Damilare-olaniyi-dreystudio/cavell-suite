# Cavell Suites website plan

## Implementation
React + Vite with React Router, Framer Motion and Lucide React. Five direct routes (`/`, `/rooms`, `/dining`, `/experiences`, `/contact`) share a responsive header, footer, enquiry flow and reveal primitives. Supplied imagery and video are copied to `public/assets`; the route manifest is served from `public/manus-routes.json`. The contact form validates required fields and opens a URL-encoded WhatsApp enquiry to `https://wa.me/2349075593974`.

## Design
- **Design movement:** editorial architectural hospitality with an asymmetric magazine rhythm.
- **Core principles:** confident type, quiet negative space, tactile image cropping, warm restrained contrast.
- **Color philosophy:** Deep Canopy anchors trust and privacy; Warm Limestone gives the site breathing room; Terracotta marks decisive moments without resorting to gold.
- **Layout paradigm:** offset columns, full-bleed media, and alternating split sections rather than repeated card grids.
- **Signature elements:** oversized numerals, thin rule dividers, and a terracotta location marker.
- **Interaction philosophy:** transitions feel like turning a page; actions remain obvious and low-friction.
- **Animation:** short opacity/transform reveals, restrained hover lifts, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- **Typography:** Manrope throughout, with display weights for headlines and regular/medium body copy.
- **Brand essence:** a design-led urban retreat for guests who value privacy, perspective and considered hospitality. Personality: assured, warm, observant.
- **Voice:** direct, warm, understated. Example lines: “A different perspective on staying in Lagos.” / “Every detail is considered.”
- **Wordmark:** spaced uppercase wordmark paired with a small architectural monogram line.
- **Signature brand color:** Deep Canopy `#203B35`.
